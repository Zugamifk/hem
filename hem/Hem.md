Hem is a land recently settled by the refugees from the great war in the east. It lies in the northwestern region of [[Manable]]. To the south, across [[Duuk's Bay]], lies the kingdom of [[Pyz]]. To the east lies the North Great Range, a massive mountain range that crosses the northern half of Manable, separating Hem and Pyz from the kingdoms in the east.

Hem has been two large cities of settlers, several castle fortifications, and numerous smaller towns and cities. The Pethraicheann is the first major settlement of refugees, a pair of cities separated by a large river. It lies on the coast coast of Duuk's Bay, at the mouth of the river, and it is where most  of the settlers live. The land of Hem is mostly unexplored and many unknown dangers make travelling outside the city limits dangerous for most travelers. Ulsheskeil is the second city and lies inland, near the center of Hem. It is build around a large mesa surrounded by hills and lowlands. The mesa has an ancient castle fortification built on top, built by a previous civilization, and has been partially rebuilt and refurbished by the new settlers. This castle houses most of the nobility and many artisans and merchants, who find it a safe place away from large populace living in the Pethraicheann. The city surrounding the castle is mostly populated by farmers, craftsmen, guilds, and traders, who manage a local economy and run caravans to the Pethraicheann.

In the west of Hem is a large forest. This forest has been mostly unexplored, and it is believed to be protected by some kind of magic. Many explorers have gotten lost, and those who have returned have told stories of strange visions, nightmarish creatures, and some have even talked about meeting creatures who spoke to them and warned them to stay away.

In the far western point of Hem lies a fortress. It's believed this fortress was built by the same civilization that built castle Ulsheskeil, and its massive tower serves as a watch point for incoming ships.

In the far north is a massive escarpment, on top of which is a massive frozen desert, perpetually covered in glaciers and massive dunes of snow. This flatland is inhabited by massive centipede-like creatures that tunnel through the ice and snow and often burst through the ground to ambush travelers and other creatures as prey. There are also many giants who travel the land. No one has managed to communicate with them and they seem completely uninterested in human travelers. At the northern coast of this flatland is [[Castle Umete]], another ancient castle that is home to a guild house from the old old in the east. This guild only attracts travelers brave enough to handle the journey through the frozen flatlands and otherwise lies completely isolated from the settler cities to the south.

To the east, most of the terrain is hilly, and full of lakes and a few small forests. Further east, the land becomes a flat grassland for hundreds of miles, before abruptly meeting the massive, intractable mountains of the North Great Range. A few paths lead into the mountains, but no road has been able to fully cross them, as a massive ridge through the range acts as a practical barrier to travelers. One notable site in the mountains is the [[Horidas Rogin]], a massive pillar of stone nearly half a mile across, etched with runes and surrounded by an abandoned temple complex. A small group of scholars and monks has settled here, and their focus is on archeological study and investigation of the complex and the pillar. 

Towards the south the landscape transforms to badlands and eventually meets the desert of Pyz, where it borders. This border is defined by a massive wall, and heavily guarded by the enigmatic people of Pyz. These people to not speak, or acknowledge the existence of the settlers. They do not respond to hostility and seem impervious to any harm from the settler weapons, so the settlers have stayed away, and no new settlements have been built near the border. There is no political relationship, trade, or communication with the kingdom of Pyz.

In the center of Duuk's Bay lies [[Duuk's Island]], a large ridge island sloping out of the ocean and stretching about 25 miles from east to west. This island is inhabited by two people, a hermit from the Pethraicheann and a young scholar who is his student. Not much is known about this island, but its shape and geology are  remarkably different from the rest of the mainland and so the scholar has focused her studies on understanding its origin and history.
